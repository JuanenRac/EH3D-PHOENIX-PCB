# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- Add the crate `eh3d-phoenix-sim`, a circuit simulator of our own for linear circuits and circuits with diodes, with no dependency beyond the complex numbers of the RF crate. The netlist in memory has a reader and a writer of SPICE-style text (`R C L V I`, the dependent sources `E G F H`, coupled inductors `K`, diodes with the Shockley equation, `.param` with expressions between braces, `.model`, simple `.subckt`, the unit suffixes `f p n u m k meg g t mil`, sources with `DC`, `AC`, `SIN`, `PULSE` and `PWL`) whose errors have a code and say the line, and what it writes reads back as the same netlist. The analyses are modified nodal analysis with a solver written for the crate (Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting, real and complex, that names the unknown a singular matrix leaves undetermined): the DC operating point with Newton-Raphson for diodes (voltage limiting, source stepping as a fallback, a report of nodes with no path to ground), AC sweeps (linear, per decade, per octave) with magnitude, phase and group delay, and transient analysis (backward Euler and trapezoidal, fixed step or a simple adaptive step, initial conditions). The netlist of a schematic is made from its nets, the values of its parts and the nets that mean ground, with a report of every part and pin it could not translate. A coherence check simulates a ladder filter of the RF crate and compares magnitude, phase and group delay with its ABCD response. Results are deterministic CSV and JSON tables that say they are an own numerical approximation. Every limit, tolerance and default is in `config/sim.cfg`; the codes are `PHOENIX-SIM-001` to `PHOENIX-SIM-021` with titles in the eight languages. It is a prototype tested with values of the textbooks (divider, RC time constant, low-pass corner, series RLC with its resonance and Q, half-wave rectifier, transformer from coupled inductors) and with properties (superposition, conservation of current, repeatable results); it has not been compared with SPICE or with a measurement.
- Add the command `phoenix sim` (`--op`, `--ac`, `--tran`, `--netlist`, `--check-filter`, `--probe`, `--out`, `--json`, `--config`, `--lang`): simulates a netlist or the schematic of a project and writes CSV or JSON, or checks a ladder filter of the RF tools against its simulation.
- Document the simulator in `docs/SIM.md`, the provisional decision to write a solver of our own in `docs/adr/0010-own-circuit-simulator.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`, the codes in `docs/ERROR_CODES.md`, the state of requirement PPCB-022 (prototype), the progress of phase F12 in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, the open question in `docs/STATUS.md`, the ideas left for later in `docs/NOT_YET.md`, and the README files in the eight languages and the two pictures.
- Bring the test counters up to date: 952 tests of the Rust code (counted from the sources) and 3 of the base.
- Add the crate `eh3d-phoenix-erc` and the command `phoenix erc` (`--config`, `--json`, `--lang`): the electrical rule check of a schematic in a fixed order with stable codes `PHOENIX-ERC-001` to `PHOENIX-ERC-016`: pins on no net, nets with a single pin, pin types that a matrix does not allow together, inputs with no driver, power inputs with no source, net names that look alike (`GND` and `GND1`), reference designators used twice or empty, empty values, parts without a footprint, symbol pins without a number or with a repeated one, and wires and junctions that join no pin. The level of every rule and the whole matrix of pin types are in `config/erc.cfg`, which is also the built-in default. The report keeps the confirmed and the inconclusive findings apart, as text in the eight languages and as JSON, and a pin without a type is left out of the checks that need one and said so.

- Add the crate `eh3d-phoenix-sync` and the command `phoenix sync` (`--backward`, `--apply --out`, `--package`, `--json`, `--lang`, `--config`): forward annotation (the transfer of a schematic to a board) and backward annotation by the identity of the parts, with codes `PHOENIX-SYNC-001` to `PHOENIX-SYNC-015`. The changes (new parts, parts that are gone, reference designators, values, footprints, nets and the nets of the pads) are a package in a fixed order that is planned as a dry run, applied as one step of the undo/redo history of the board or of the schematic, undone and redone exactly, and applied a second time with no effect; nothing the package does not name is touched. ## [0.0.1] - The base of the repository

- **The base that every repository of the suite has:** the manifest, the README files in eight languages, this changelog, the CI workflow, the issue and pull-request templates, and the shared checks of EH3D-DOCS (the repository check and the drawers of the two README pictures).
- **A planned project, described honestly:** the scope, the requirements (all planned), the relations with other repositories, the threat model, the decisions that apply and the rules for writing code are in `docs/`; there is no code yet.
