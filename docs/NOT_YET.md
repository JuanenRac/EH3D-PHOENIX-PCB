# EH3D-PHOENIX-PCB: not yet

Good ideas that are not in the first version. Writing them here avoids implementing them early.

## What to do first, by value for work on real EAGLE projects

The table below is in the order the ideas were written down. This is the order to take them in when real projects are the aim, from what stops a real board from going through the suite to what only makes it nicer:

1. Slots as a shape of the model (the cutouts are kept now, and so are the arcs of the outline, `docs/OUTLINE_ARCS.md`; the arcs of the cutouts are still cut into straight pieces, and no editor makes an arc of the outline).
2. Chamfered, trapezoidal and custom pads, and slots, in the model (the roundness and the plating of a pad are in the model now): the Gerber and Excellon files cannot say what the model cannot hold.
3. The join of the other pads of its own net in a pour made by the export itself, and the removal of the islands that the clearing cuts off inside one zone (the cutouts, the keep-away around other nets, the reliefs of round pads with two or four spokes and those of rectangular, rounded-corner and oblong pads are cleared with clear polarity now, and a zone or a group of zones smaller than a minimum is left out).
4. Back drilling (the drill files of blind and buried vias are written, one for each pair of layers).
5. The priority and the relief of a zone in the model instead of the data of the editor, so that the KiCad and Specctra writers can carry them (the cutouts are in the model now).
6. The drawing of the bus, the buses in the EAGLE writer, and the join of the members through bus labels between sheets (the KiCad reader and writer and the EAGLE reader carry the buses and their entries now).
7. The binary EAGLE libraries and real files of makers and users in the corpus, so that the rates of reading are measured on what people have.
8. The window of the library mode and the selection of several items; the operations behind them are done.

Done from this list: the copy of a footprint, a symbol and a device set, the move, copy, layer and width of several items of a library, the pads of a via on every layer it spans in the Gerber files and in their check, the time of a check, the cutouts of the outline, the roundness and the plating of a pad, the cutouts and the keep-away of a pour with clear polarity, the drill files of blind and buried vias, the buses of KiCad and EAGLE schematics as objects (the EAGLE writer reports them), the cutouts of the boards in a panel, the cutouts of a zone in the KiCad, EAGLE and Specctra files, the names of the new steps in the history of the window, an existing via of the net as a free change of layer, differential pairs routed as two coupled tracks on one layer, four-spoke thermal reliefs of round pads in the Gerber files, two-spoke reliefs and reliefs of rectangular and oblong pads with the width and the gap of the profile, and a minimum area for the islands of a pour in the Gerber files.
